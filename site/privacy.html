Privacy policy

BNP Scorer, by Bits N Pixs. Effective 17 November 2026. Updated the same day for sign-in, live matches, tournaments and scheduled matches, and on 18 November 2026 for pasting and importing teams.

The short version

BNP Scorer keeps your matches on your phone. You can use all of it without an account. If you choose to sign in with Google, the new matches, tournaments and scheduled matches you create are published so anyone with the app can follow them live. Entry fee payments never leave your phone. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell anything.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Matches you score, ball by ball, the tournaments you create, and the matches you schedule for later.
  • Team and player names you type in.
  • For a tournament: its prizes, rules, entry fee, and what each team has paid, if you record it.
  • Your choice of light or dark theme.
  • A short list of recent app errors, so you can send them to us if you choose (see "Problem reports" below).

All of it lives in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Signing in and publishing (optional)

Signing in is optional and off until you turn it on in Settings › Account. Before you sign in, the app tells you what becomes public.

When you sign in with Google, Google tells the app your name, email address and an account id. We use Google's Firebase Authentication to keep you signed in.

While you are signed in, every new match you score, tournament you create and match you schedule for later is published to our database (Google Cloud Firestore, in Mumbai, India), and kept up to date as it changes.

  • A match: the two teams and their player names, the toss, the overs and the ground, if you entered one; every ball, as scored, with the time it was recorded.
  • A tournament: its name, teams and player names, the format and draw, fixtures with their dates and grounds, and the prizes, entry fee and rules you wrote.
  • A scheduled match: the two teams and their player names, the overs, the time and the ground.
  • With each: your name from your Google account, shown as "Scored by" or "Organised by", and your account id.

Who has paid the entry fee is never published. It stays on your phone, and our database refuses a tournament that carries it.

What you publish is public. Anyone with BNP Scorer can see and follow it, without signing in. Your email address is never published or shown to anyone.

What you created before signing in, or create after signing out, stays on your phone. Only you can change or remove what you published.

Removing what you published

  • One match or tournament: delete it in the app (hold it on the Matches or Tournaments tab). That removes it from our database too.
  • A scheduled match: cancel it, or start it; either removes it.
  • Stop publishing: sign out in Settings › Account. What you already published stays public until you delete it.
  • Your account and everything you published: ask us (see "Contact"), and we delete them within 30 days. Please do not post your email address in a public issue.

We keep what you published until it is deleted. We do not keep copies of anything deleted.

Who else handles your data

Google runs our sign-in and database (Firebase), and handles that data for us under the Firebase privacy and security terms. Like any web service, it sees the network address of the phone connecting to it. Data travels encrypted. We share it with no one else.

Your phone's own backup

If you have turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android may include BNP Scorer's data in your phone's backup to your Google account. That backup is between you and Google. Bits N Pixs cannot see it.

When you share something

These leave your phone only when you choose to send them, through your phone's share sheet, to the app and people you pick:

  • Scorecards: a picture of a scorecard with a caption.
  • Backups: a file with your matches, teams and tournaments, including the entry fee payments you recorded, for keeping safe or moving to a new phone.
  • Invites: a message with the teams, time and ground of an upcoming match.
  • Team template: a CSV file with two example teams, to fill in with your own. It holds nothing of yours.
  • Problem reports: the app version, your phone's operating-system version, recent error messages and anything you write. You see the full text before you send it.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to sign in, publish your matches and show other people's. Scoring works without a connection; published matches catch up when you are back online.
  • Biometric and fingerprint: listed because Google's sign-in library includes them. BNP Scorer never asks for or uses your fingerprint or face.

No location, contacts, camera, microphone or phone storage. When you restore a backup or import teams, the app reads only the file you pick. When you open Paste players, it reads the text you last copied, to fill in the list for you to check; that text stays on your phone, and only the names you add are kept.

Other people's names

Matches, tournaments and scheduled matches contain the names of the players you enter. If you are signed in, those names are published with them, so let your players know. A player who wants their name removed can ask the scorer to delete the match, or ask us.

Children

BNP Scorer is not directed at children under 13, and signing in is not meant for them. If you believe a child has published a match, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change what the app collects or publishes, we update this policy before that version is released, and the app tells you. The effective date above shows the latest change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/BitsNPixs/bnp-scorer/issues, or use Settings › Report a problem in the app. For a deletion request, give the name shown as "Scored by" and one of the matches; never your email address.
