Privacy policy

BNP Scorer, by Bits N Pixs. Effective 17 November 2026.

The short version

BNP Scorer keeps everything on your phone. It has no account, no internet access, no ads and no analytics. Bits N Pixs never receives your matches, teams or any personal information.

What the app stores, and where

  • Matches you score, ball by ball, and the tournaments you create.
  • Team and player names you type in.
  • Your choice of light or dark theme.
  • A short list of recent app errors, so you can send them to us if you choose (see "Problem reports" below).

All of it lives in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Your phone's own backup

If you have turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android may include BNP Scorer's data in your phone's backup to your Google account. That backup is between you and Google. Bits N Pixs cannot see it.

When something leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it, through your phone's share sheet, to the app and people you pick:

  • Scorecards: a picture of a scorecard with a caption.
  • Backups: a file with your matches, teams and tournaments, for keeping safe or moving to a new phone.
  • Problem reports: the app version, your phone's operating-system version, recent error messages and anything you write. You see the full text before you send it.

Permissions

BNP Scorer asks for no special permissions: no internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or phone storage. When you restore a backup, it reads only the file you pick.

Other people's names

The player names you enter stay on your phone, unless you share a scorecard or backup that contains them.

Children

BNP Scorer is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If a future version adds online features, such as live scores or accounts, we will update this policy before that version is released, and the app will tell you. The effective date above shows the latest change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/BitsNPixs/bnp-scorer/issues, or use Settings › Report a problem in the app.
